# Customer User Manual — Sweet Gonz Kiosk

## 1. Start

1. The tablet shows the **Welcome** screen with the Sweet Gonz logo.
2. Choose your language: **English** or **Filipino** (English is the
   default).
3. Tap **Start Order / Simulan ang Order**.

> Starting a new order clears any previous order from the screen.

## 2. Pick your items

- Browse categories (Pasta, Snacks, Bread, Drip Coffee, Espresso,
  Ice-Shaken Drinks, Non-Coffee Drinks) or use the **search** box.
- Each item shows its picture, name, description, and price.
- Tap **Add / Idagdag** (or **Customize / I-customize**) on an item.
- Items marked **Sold out / Ubos na** cannot be ordered.

## 3. Customize (optional)

- Choose **required choices** when shown (e.g. Crinkled Fries flavor:
  Cheese or Sour Cream) — you cannot add the item until you choose.
- Choose any **add-ons** (extra shot, syrup, etc.). Prices update live.
- Set the **quantity** (1 to 20).
- The **line total** updates as you go.
- Tap **Add to cart / Idagdag sa cart**.

## 4. Review your order

- Open the cart: the panel on the right (wide screens) or the cart button
  at the bottom (narrow screens).
- Adjust quantities, remove items, or clear the cart.
- Tap **Review order / Suriin ang order**, check everything, then tap
  **Continue to payment / Magpatuloy sa pagbabayad**.

## 5. Pay

You have two choices:

**Cash** — Tap the Cash button. Your order is created and you pay the
total at the counter. A staff member confirms your payment.

**Demo E-Wallet (simulated)** — Tap Demo E-Wallet. A demo QR and a
reference like `DEMO-3F9A2C81` are shown. **This is a demonstration only:
no real money moves, no real payment happens.** Tap the button to record
the order as a simulated e-wallet payment.

## 6. Confirmation and receipt

- Your **order number** is shown large (e.g. `SG-20260806-001`).
- Keep it for pickup.
- The screen shows your items, totals, and payment status.
- Tap **Print receipt / I-print ang resibo** if staff ask for a copy.
- Tap **Finish / Tapos** when done — or wait; the screen resets
  automatically after 20 seconds for the next customer.

## 7. If something goes wrong

| Situation                              | What to do                                                                                                                        |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The screen asks "Are you still there?" | Tap **I'm still here / Nandito pa ako** to keep your order.                                                                       |
| The kiosk resets                       | Your unfinished order is cleared after 120 s of inactivity — start again.                                                         |
| "Connection lost" message              | The kiosk cannot reach the server. Your cart is saved. Tell a staff member; ordering stays disabled until the connection returns. |
| The page refreshes by accident         | Your cart is restored automatically.                                                                                              |
| An item says it is sold out at payment | The item ran out after you added it; your cart is cleared and you can choose another item.                                        |
| Payment fails with an error            | Try again once; if it still fails, tell staff. Never tap repeatedly.                                                              |

## 8. Privacy

- The kiosk does not ask for names, numbers, or any personal information.
- Orders are saved only on the shop's local server for fulfillment and
  daily reporting.
